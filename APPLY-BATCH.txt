RondiTrack Assignment 10.1 consolidated mobile batch

Copy the contents of this archive into the root of your existing RondiTrack
repository, preserving the mobile/ and .github/ paths. Allow files to overwrite.
Do not copy any source ZIP or context TXT files into Git.

Then, from the repository root:
  git status --short
  cd mobile
  ./run-assignment-10-1-checks.sh

The source was prepared in a Linux container without Flutter/Dart or Xcode.
The script regenerates Freezed/Riverpod outputs before analysis. Review and
commit the regenerated *.freezed.dart and *.g.dart files after the script runs.

Known not-complete items are explicitly listed in mobile/README.md. In
particular, iOS schemes/bundle identifiers require Xcode on macOS, and the
assignment's full detail models/screens, family stale-frame tests, provider
disposal proof, and red/green CI evidence still need completion. Do not claim
those are complete based on this batch.
